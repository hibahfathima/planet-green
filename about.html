Environmental expertise for Land,Sea and Everything in Between

Planet Green provides integrated environmental consultancy and technical services to support sustainable development. Our multidisciplinary capabilities cover environmental assessment, regulatory compliance, marine and terrestrial surveys, environmental modelling, greenhouse gas reporting, and practical mitigation solutions

Meet the Team

Behind every assessment is a team that's spent years in the field, walking sites, running the models, and sitting across the table from regulators to get projects approved. Our consultants hold doctorates in environmental science and related disciplines, but what matters more is the work: EIAs, permitting, and monitoring plans delivered across energy, mining, transportation, and industrial development projects throughout the Gulf region.
