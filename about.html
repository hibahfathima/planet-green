About

Over 20 years in environmental consulting

We provide comprehensive Environmental Impact Assessments that identify potential environmental risks and opportunities before a project begins. Our team evaluates impacts on land, water, air, biodiversity, and surrounding communities.

Our assessments help clients understand regulatory requirements, reduce environmental risks, and move projects forward with confidence while meeting local and international standards.

Our modeling and technical studies provide reliable data for understanding how proposed developments may affect the surrounding environment. We use technical analysis to evaluate air quality, noise, water systems, and other environmental factors.

These studies give project teams the technical evidence needed to make informed decisions, improve project design, and meet environmental approval requirements.

We support clients throughout the environmental permitting process, from identifying applicable requirements to preparing technical documentation and supporting regulatory submissions.

Our team works closely with project stakeholders to make the approval process clear and efficient, helping projects satisfy environmental regulations and progress toward implementation.

Our monitoring and compliance services help businesses maintain environmental performance throughout the life of a project. We regularly evaluate operations against approved environmental requirements and agreed performance standards.

From ongoing monitoring to compliance reporting, we help clients identify issues early, take corrective action, and maintain responsible environmental practices.

Meet the Team

Behind every assessment is a team that's spent years in the field, walking sites, running the models, and sitting across the table from regulators to get projects approved. Our consultants hold doctorates in environmental science and related disciplines, but what matters more is the work: EIAs, permitting, and monitoring plans delivered across energy, mining, transportation, and industrial development projects throughout the Gulf region.
